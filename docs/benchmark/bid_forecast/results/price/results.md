## Limitations

- Replay: our wins do not change manager state (frequency or volume), a standard assumption in offline RTB evaluation.
- v does not account for our campaign frequency caps or volumes; it reflects targeting and CPM only.
- Manager prices lie on a discrete CPM ladder, so Cui's log-normal model is necessarily an approximation.
- Censored mode: on a win we observe z (feedback gives the minimum winning bid); on a loss we only know z ≥ b.
