## Simplifications and features

- Only PRECISE-matching (fuzzy and parasitic-limits do not apply).
- `auction/simulation`: active (start/end) → ad_frequency_limit (t/h/d/w/m sliding windows) → user_frequency_limit → min_interval → precise match → winner selection.
- `min_interval` reads from `ad_frequency_limit` (as does the `get_ad_min_interval` SDK) and applies to the user×ad pair.
- velocity/heuristics/hybrid: month_limit = `get_real_month_volume(ad)` from the SDK, current value = impressions for moving 30 days.
- Volume is a hard limit of impressions (as in a synthetic benchmark); budget = volume × cpm (required for A3).
- User = client_id (hash uint32). Tags: Region, Device, Child, Category (VOD), Channel (live); Category and Channel are mutually exclusive; rare values are folded in `<other>`.
- Adwords (a2/a3) run once (not N runs) on a pool of candidates who have passed the filters.
- Balancer: `balance_traffic` on the empirical probabilities of tags from logs, then weight = distribution × P(req) / P(ad) on the pool of candidates who have passed the filters.
